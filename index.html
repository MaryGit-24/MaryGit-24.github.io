About me
Machine learning learns the way I do: patterns, practice, feedback. That is when data stopped being abstract for me.

I studied Health Education at Ambrose Alli University, found my way into tech through the Women Techsters Fellowship by Tech4Dev, and never looked back. Since then I have trained in machine learning with MIT Universal AI and in computer vision at NECA ICT.

By day I am a Quantitative Insights Researcher, designing questionnaires, checking field data and turning it into reports clients act on. Through SME Ally, I build data and AI tools for small businesses: a data solution when the problem is data, an AI tool when the work is repetitive.

My faith guides all of it. And the part I love most is teaching.
